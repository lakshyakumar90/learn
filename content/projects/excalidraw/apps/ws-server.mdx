The server speaks two protocols over one socket family: presence frames and
collaboration frames, each validated and rate-limited. Commits are applied through
the shared collaboration service and acknowledged per mutation; previews and
selections are fanned out to the room and never stored. One connection entry
represents one tab, never one user.
