Guest drawings stay on the device in IndexedDB. Signed-in work goes through the
room sync orchestrator, which keeps a shared room connection, an IndexedDB draft,
and a persistent outbox of committed edits. Committed edits are durable; cursor
positions, previews, selections, and laser trails are ephemeral frames that are
never stored.
