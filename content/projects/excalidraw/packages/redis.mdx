Redis is the live authority and Postgres the durable one. Snapshot writes are
atomic with a Lua version check; when a write lands, the room joins the dirty set
and a coalesced flush job is scheduled — debounced, with a maximum wait, and forced
when the last participant leaves. Rate budgets are per connection for ephemeral
frames and stricter for commits.
