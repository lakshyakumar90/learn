Messages, comments, and job applications are embedded subdocuments, not
separate collections. Passwords hash on save with cost 12 and never leave
the database (the field is excluded from queries by default). The chat
model drops near-duplicate messages at save time and keeps the last-message
timestamp fresh for conversation ordering.
