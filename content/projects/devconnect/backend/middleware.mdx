The guard reads the `jwt` cookie first and falls back to the Authorization
header — that fallback is what makes hosted frontends work where
third-party cookies are blocked. Limits range from 15 auth attempts per 15
minutes to 60 searches per 5 minutes. Uploads pick Cloudinary storage when
keys exist and local disk otherwise, cap files at 10MB, and convert Multer
errors into 400 responses instead of crashes.
