`database.js` connects at import time and exits the process on failure —
there is no retry. `redis.js` creates three lazy clients (presence,
publish, subscribe) and stops retrying after five failures, letting the app
run without Redis. `cloudinary.js` configures only when all three keys are
present; otherwise uploads fall back to the local folder.
