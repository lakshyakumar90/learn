The `ui/` subfolder follows the shadcn pattern (configured in
`components.json`, new-york style): Radix primitives styled with the `cn`
helper. Feature widgets stay near their feature — posts, jobs, and the bell
each own their modals and cards — while layout and the route guard frame
every guarded screen.
