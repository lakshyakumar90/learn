`public/` carries the favicon, the social preview image, and the Vite badge
— files the build copies untouched. `src/assets/` holds only the default
Vite logo; the app has no other bundled artwork. User content (avatars,
post images, resumes) never lives here — it goes to Cloudinary or the
backend `uploads/` folder.
