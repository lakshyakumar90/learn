Pages are thin screens over services and slices: forms dispatch thunks,
lists select slice state, and the chat page wires the socket service for
typing, presence, and receipts. Note `pages/auth/` holds older Login and
Signup screens that nothing imports — the router uses the top-level
`LoginPage` and `SignupPage` instead.
