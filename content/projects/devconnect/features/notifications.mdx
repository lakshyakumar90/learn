Every notification is a stored row first — connection requests and accepts,
likes, comments, job applications, and new messages — so nothing is lost
when the recipient is offline. The same helper then pushes into the
recipient room for instant display. Self-actions never notify. The bell
catches up over REST with unread counts; live arrivals land through the
socket service into the slice.
