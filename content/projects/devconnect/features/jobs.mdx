List views strip applications down to counts and an applied flag; only the
poster sees full applications on the detail view. Applying attaches a
resume plus up to five extra documents through the document uploader and
notifies the poster. Active job pairs (poster plus pending, reviewed, or
accepted applicants) may message each other without a connection.
