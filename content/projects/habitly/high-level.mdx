Screens render feature UI, feature UI updates app state, app state persists
through repositories into SQLite. Reminder times flow sideways into the
device layer, which reconciles them with OS alarms. Nothing leaves the
device unless a signed-in account exists — then the outbox carries edits to
Firestore and listeners bring other devices' edits back.

Solid lines are on-device data; dashed lines are optional cloud sync, only
active for signed-in accounts with Firebase configured.
