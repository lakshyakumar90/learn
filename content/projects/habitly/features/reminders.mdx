All reminders are local and scheduled — no push service exists. Times and
OS identifiers persist in SQLite; the reconcile pass schedules what is
missing and drops what is stale whenever data, accounts, or sync change.
Habit reminders repeat weekly, tasks use date triggers, and two optional
daily nudges cover summary and motivation.
